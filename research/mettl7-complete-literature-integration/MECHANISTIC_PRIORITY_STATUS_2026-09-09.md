# METTL7 mechanistic literature priority status

## Netarsudil reconciliation

Chen did not perform a netarsudil catalytic assay. Its operational activator label is supported by SPR signal, cellular METTL7B-expression induction, phenotype, and knockout dependence. Fig 8E reports `KD = 9.53 mM`, but the maximum analyte concentration was 100 uM; the fit is therefore about 95-fold outside the experimental range and is poorly constrained. Dr. Rheem's separate direct TSL endpoint gives the current authoritative METTL7B IC50 of approximately 40 uM and no detectable A inhibition. The context difference is unresolved, not explained.

## Thiol corpus

The 2024 HDAC-thiol paper is fully integrated. Reduced romidepsin is a direct purified-enzyme positive for both A and B (SAM-dependent LC-MS monomethylated product). Largazole, OKI-005, KD5170, and NCH-51 remain cellular pharmacology observations unless separately tested biochemically. The conserved-TMT1A paper establishes cellular dimethylated-romidepsin formation by five TMT1A orthologs, not purified turnover.
