# METTL7 RNA catalysis audit

## Verdict

`DIRECT_METTL7A_m7G = GRADE_A_WITH_POLYPEPTIDE_ATTRIBUTION_CAVEAT`

The Shentu preparation transfers isotope-labelled methyl from d3-SAM to defined
KLF4 and NFKBIA transcripts and produces chemically resolved d3-m7G by
LC-MS/MS. This establishes methyl transfer to guanosine, formation of m7G, and
dependence on a METTL7A-containing preparation. It does not identify the exact
modified guanosine or exclude catalysis by a tightly copurifying protein: the
HA-METTL7A preparation came from HEK293T cells, purity was assessed by gel, and
the reaction lacked a matched mock purification, purified catalytic mutant,
activity-through-fractionation experiment, or quantitative multiple-turnover
demonstration.

`DIRECT_METTL7A_m6A = GRADE_B`

The older LOC606724/SNHG1 study used purified His-tagged full-length and
truncated METTL7A, supplied SAM, and reported methyltransferase-kit and anti-m6A
signals on defined RNA oligonucleotides. It is biochemical evidence, but no
isotope-resolved or nucleotide-resolved m6A product was demonstrated. The
reported 76-172 active interval is a deletion inference, not a catalytic-site
map.

CORIN, CIDEC, BMP2, SLC1A5, PLSCR1/CXCR4, LOC606724/SNHG1 cellular effects and
other transcript claims must not be promoted merely because METTL7A
perturbation changes MeRIP signal, RNA stability, translation or phenotype.
Except for the biochemical result above, these are Grade C or D for direct
catalysis. None independently demonstrates METTL7A-polypeptide transfer of a
methyl group to adenine N6.
