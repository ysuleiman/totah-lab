# Protocol comparability

Comparability is explicit and precedes reuse, fitting, or comparison. Protocol keys retain method, basis, dispersion, environment, counterpoise state, software, and version.

Prometheus distinguishes comparable evidence, reference-shift-compatible evidence, the same geometry at a different method, incompatible energy targets, incompatible protocols, and incomplete metadata. In particular, PBE-D3(BJ)/def2-SVP conformational evidence, PBE0/def2-TZVP counterpoise interactions, and HF/6-31G(d) ESP evidence are different scientific targets and cannot be merged silently.

Constraints and requested outputs are part of the calculation contract. Evidence generated with the same method but a different contract is not reused.
