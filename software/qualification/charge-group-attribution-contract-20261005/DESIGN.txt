SCIENTIFIC DEFINITION PROPOSED FOR BOUNDED QUALIFICATION
Describe the explicitly supplied formal charge of an already-qualified source group.
Initial domain only:
  ATHENA.GROUP.CARBOXYLATE and CARBOXYLATE.FORMYL (/1 definitions)
  ATHENA.GROUP.AMMONIUM.PRIMARY_CARBON_BOUND, SECONDARY_CARBON_BOUND,
  TERTIARY_CARBON_BOUND, QUATERNARY_CARBON_BOUND (/2 definitions).
Reuse exact existing queries/state domains; do not copy them into new patterns.
For every verified source occurrence, sum authoritative integer formal charges over
its exact memberAtomIds, once per distinct member, using overflow-checked arithmetic.
Carboxylate's C/O/O membership remains distinct from any future oxygen-only geometric
representative. Ammonium's source member is N, with C/H context retained in source.
The sum describes the declared group boundary, not charge localization/delocalization,
partial charge, pKa, protonation inference, physiological ionization, salt policy,
whole-component net charge, interaction center, electrostatic energy or affinity.
Nitro is not promoted from its internally charged atoms to either supported group.
Other charged motifs, arbitrary charge magnitudes and resonance encodings remain
outside this initial group domain. Neutral acid/amine controls are not assertions of
nonionizability. Contradictory source states coexist in separate state-bound reports.
