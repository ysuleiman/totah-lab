DEFINITION
Let P,Q be the ordered supplied points; G is the independently computed equal-weight
centroid of the complete supplied group. Preserve the explicit tuple/group/source pins.
Proposed quantity keys:
- centroidAngstrom: G (ANGSTROM; 3-vector).
- firstCentroidDistanceAngstrom: |P-G| (ANGSTROM).
- secondCentroidDistanceAngstrom: |Q-G| (ANGSTROM).
- firstSecondCentroidAngleDegrees: angle(P-Q, G-Q) (DEGREE; [0,180]).
- secondCentroidNormalAngleDegrees: acos(|n dot (Q-G)| / |Q-G|)
  in degrees [0,90], for a normalized, qualified unique group-plane normal n only.
Clamp floating-point cosine to its mathematical domain; reuse Gaia vector/plane math.
Existing POINT_PLANE supplies height/offset; existing pair matrix supplies closest atoms.
Do not duplicate those outputs or add a new coordinate/frame authority.
These labels describe geometry only; P/Q are not inherently C/H or a chemical donor.
