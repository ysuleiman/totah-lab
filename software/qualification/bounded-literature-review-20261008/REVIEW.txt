COMMON NORMATIVE BOUNDARY
Each candidate concerns an explicitly selected atom/group tuple in one immutable
source state and coordinate frame. Source facts must independently establish
identity, formal charge, H inventory, aromaticity, exact ordinary/nonordinary
connections, electronic NONE and correspondence. A scope-completeness assertion
proves coverage, not chemistry. Prepared H must carry preparation lineage; explicit
coordinates are not automatically experimentally observed coordinates. Do not
place H, choose a tautomer, repair bonds, or infer protonation during detection.
Contradictions remain inconclusive; known exclusions are unsupported; absent
scientific authority is NOT_EVALUATED. Missing facts never become false negatives.
Selected-pair negatives require complete eligibility and geometry for that tuple.
System-wide absence additionally requires an exhaustive candidate universe and
eligible-geometry coverage, including symmetry and alternate-state scope. No such
global absence is proposed here. Different explicit H tuples remain distinct.
Every derived result binds source bytes, source/preparation protocol, state,
atom/group identities, geometry plan, definition version and independent Gate
authority. No favorable energy, affinity, potency, biological importance, mechanism
or catalytic competence follows from any positive geometry below.

I04-A — EXPLICIT ALIPHATIC C–H / AROMATIC PI SURVEY CANDIDATE
Dependency chain: source/state + correspondence -> complete C/H and ring identities
-> P03/P09 bounded chemistry + group geometry -> selected H-centroid distance ->
I04-specific predicate/coverage -> Research Gate. I02 explicit-H qualification is
not qualification of this different interaction; I03-A proxies cannot supply H.
Primary source: 10.1021/acs.jctc.3c00300, pinned PMC10448718.
Source survey uses H-centroid <=3.5 A, and excludes methyl groups in H-based
analysis. The development subset additionally requires theta1<35 and theta2<35;
these are two different vector/normal angles, not C-H-centroid>145. Source H were
added with AutoDockTools; its survey also filtered occupancy/B factors. Adopting
the selected geometry does not reproduce that preparation or population survey.
Proposed first bounded domain: neutral nonaromatic saturated CH or CH2 carbon,
four ordinary single-bond valence units including its authoritative H inventory,
selected explicitly mapped covalent H; no methyl, sp/sp2, radical, aromatic C-H,
unknown charge/H or metal-bound donor. This is a restricted source-graph class,
not a universal weak donor or inferred acidity/hybridization classifier.
Select a chemically qualified Phe six-carbon ring first, with the existing exact
G03 Phe ring/backbone restrictions and uniform isotope descriptor. The donor and
protein ring must have explicit source attribution; this is not arbitrary carbon
proximity. C-H coordinates and covalent identity are separately required.
Proposed proposition: finite 0<d(H,centroid)<=3.5 A, distance-only survey candidate.
Retain C-H-centroid and orientation measurements descriptively when defined.
No stricter development-set claim is made. Its exact two-angle construction and
normal sign convention would require a separate profile, not a silent tightening.
Reuse: POINT_PAIR_GROUP with [C,H] and the six ring atoms supplies the distance and
C-H-centroid angle. A leaf source-role proof is required for the new donor subset;
the existing general donor/acceptor manifests must stay unchanged. No new matcher,
path engine or public schema is demonstrated necessary. Explicit-H source admission
must be tested independently; the current Met/Phe heavy-only helper cannot simply
be widened. Status: bounded proposal, not implemented or qualified.

I04-B — PROTONATED ALIPHATIC AMINE N+-C-H...O
Dependency: explicit protonated amine and C-H state -> source-specific protein
acceptor and its complete local role geometry -> distances/angles/dihedrals ->
I04-B predicate -> coverage/Gate. Primary: 10.1038/s41598-018-36987-9,
pinned PMC6347603, Methods and Table 1 must remain distinguishable.
Donor restriction: a source N(+1), nonaromatic closed-shell protonated aliphatic
amine, ordinary single bonds to saturated carbon and H, total valence four,
at least one N-H. Select an adjacent saturated carbon and one explicit bonded H.
Exclude neutral amines, quaternary ammonium without N-H, amidinium/guanidinium,
aromatic cations, amides, sulfonamides, unknown source state and generic C-H.
Separate acceptor domains: neutral peptide/Asn/Gln amide carbonyl O; Asp/Glu
carboxylate O with explicit deprotonated state and both oxygens; neutral Ser/Thr
alcohol O; neutral Tyr phenol O. Names do not prove these roles.
Published PDB-methods profile: dCO<3.7 and dHO<2.7 A; psi=C-H-O>90 degrees for
amide/carboxylate, >105 for hydroxyl; xi=H-O-C>105 for amide, >90 for carboxylate,
105..150 for hydroxyl; alpha=N-C-O in60..160; beta=Cdonor-O-Cacceptor>95 for
amide, >90 for carboxylate,95..160 for hydroxyl. H/C elevation respectively
<70/<80 for amide and <80/<90 for carboxylate. The survey omits hydroxyl elevation.
Table 1 instead contains class-specific lower distance bounds and oriented
carboxylate xi extending to270 degrees. Do not fuse Table 1 and survey Methods.
Source elevation uses sin(theta)=sin(xi)*sin(chi), with chi the average of two
specified dihedrals; carbon elevation has the analogous beta/delta expression.
An unsigned point-plane angle is NOT automatically equivalent. Exact signed-angle
wrapping, averaging and inverse-sine convention, plus interval endpoint treatment,
must be resolved from figures/supplement/source protocol before implementation.
The source prepared H with Discovery Studio3.5 and compared Molegro6.0; those are
not observed-H evidence or an Athena preparation authority.
Status: unresolved exact role-geometry/angle semantics. No approximation or weak
donor classifier is implemented. Existing ANGLE/DIHEDRAL/DISTANCE can supply raw
quantities; scientific interpretation is the blocker, not a new generic engine.

I11 — LIGAND CARBON-BOUND Cl / PROTEIN AROMATIC PI
Dependency: explicit ligand C-Cl -> explicit protein ring and centroid -> nearest
ring-atom selection -> point-pair/group angle and distances -> I11 coverage/Gate.
Primary: Cl–pi interactions in protein–ligand complexes, PMC2442010, Figure1
(retrieved and visually inspected). Candidate d(Cl,centroid)<4.5 A and theta<140.
EXACT theta vertex is nearest ring atom R: angle(Cl,R,centroid). It is not the
C-Cl-centroid angle and not the acute ring-normal angle. Source final dataset also
used visual removal of CH-Cl cases; this two-test predicate does not claim exact
dataset reproduction or that all surviving geometries are physical Cl-pi bonds.
Proposed narrow initial domain: neutral ligand Cl with exactly one ordinary bond
to carbon, no other connections/H/radical; source-qualified Phe ring as above.
Other ring residues require their own exact role/center contracts. F/Br/I, chloride,
unknown ligand/protein attribution and nonordinary Cl connections are excluded.
For a unique nearest ring atom, reuse POINT_PAIR_GROUP [Cl,R] and the ring group;
its firstSecondCentroidAngleDegrees is theta. Record all ring distances. An exact
nearest-distance tie must not be resolved by input order: retain tied witnesses;
until a reviewed aggregation convention is explicit, ties are inconclusive.
Source characterization is abs(dcentroid-dnearest)<=0.3 A face-on, otherwise edge-on;
it is separate from the candidate and does not imply energy. HalBS F/Cl/Br/I
distributions/scores require separate exact data/protocol review and are not adopted.
No public schema/new primitive is currently required for the unique-nearest subset.
Status: bounded proposal; full protein-ring catalog and tied-nearest aggregation
remain outside it. Not implemented or qualified.
