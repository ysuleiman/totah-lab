G03 implementation-boundary notes — 2026-10-08
The normative scientific contract is CONTRACT.txt, pinned by the new manifests.
These notes explain the actual bounded admission and do not expand that contract.

Source identity and chemistry are separate: literal MET/PHE names bind ordered
source roles, while complete actual source graph, charge/H/aromaticity/NONE facts,
ordinary-connection coverage and independent source authority establish eligibility.
Raw geometry is retained without granting chemistry or current-policy authority.

The new source manifests preserve the original selected thioether and aromatic-
carbon pattern definitions and add the qualified source-H consistency profile.
Their full applicable coverage is required. In particular, the inherited carbon-
ring profile requires its declared aromatic-carbon coverage and excludes unqualified
heteroaromatic/fused domains. An otherwise plausible selected pair in a component
whose applicable source report is incomplete remains UNKNOWN. This is not a claim
of broad applicability to every protein component or every PDB water/H preparation.
A raw pattern occurrence, residue label or complete inventory is not scientific
source authority. The five I03 admission roles and all P03 roles remain unchanged.

All selected residue bonds are checked in the original source graph. Ordinary
bonds must have the stated order and no aromatic flag. The ring requires explicit
AROMATIC order. A flagged ordinary order is not rewritten into an aromatic source
edge. Plain and flagged Kekule controls are explicitly excluded by the leaf.
No mapper, matcher, graph, event, path, geometry or generic scope rule was modified.

The original group reports and all their correspondence alternatives are retained
through exact source pins. The rule evaluates one ordered eight-anchor pair; it
neither searches for all contacts nor asserts a whole-system negative/count.
Duplicate canonical atom names and nonfinite Point3D values are rejected by the
unchanged source constructors before a G03 state can be evaluated. Missing eligible
plane/direction or ambiguous correspondence remains unknown within evaluable states.

All six source ring isotope descriptors must be identical. This only supports the
stated equal nominal mass/centroid convention; it is not an observed isotope assay.
The adopted inclusive 7 A survey distance is the sole contact threshold. The acute
ring-normal angle is diagnostic; even its 90-degree case can satisfy the distance
predicate. Degeneracy of the unused CG-SD-centroid angle alone does not veto valid
SD-centroid/plane geometry. No energy, occupancy or biological interpretation follows.

Fixture review authority is engineering-only. No production policy, authority,
receipt, signing identity, expiry or default scientific activation was generated.
