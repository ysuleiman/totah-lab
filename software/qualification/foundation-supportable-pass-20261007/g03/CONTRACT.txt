G03 bounded source-survey contract — explicit definition before implementation
ATHENA.G03.SOURCE_MET_PHE_SURVEY_CONTACT/1

Scientific proposition: one explicitly selected, independently source-attributed
methionine sulfur / phenylalanine six-carbon aromatic ring pair satisfies the
L04 survey's sulfur-to-ring-center distance convention. Chalcogen-O is not this
proposition and remains separately unresolved. Adopt 0<distance<=7angstrom as
an explicitly inclusive selected contact convention; do not impose the descriptive
30–60degree preference or5.5angstrom introductory distance as additional gates.
No inferred energy, stabilization, affinity, cysteine chemistry or biological role.

Source comparison: L04 experimental procedures use the Met SD and the six-membered
aromatic ring center of mass; its results choose7angstrom from the first radial
minimum. Its energy/QM/biological results involve additional calculations and
experiments and are not labels assigned by this distance predicate. L05 sulfonium
bins and L07 chalcogen/H-bond directional classes have different state/partner roles.
They do not broaden this definition. Phe-only is an intentional subset of L04's
Phe/Tyr/Trp survey domain, avoiding fused-ring or heteroatom center ambiguity.

Exact chemistry to independently qualify:
- Source residue identity MET and PHE, exact source atom names/chain/residue/insertion
  correspondence in one immutable state; labels alone never establish chemistry.
- Met saturated neutral divalent thioether with source SD single-bonded to CG(H2)
  and CE(H3); CG single-bonded to CB(H2), CB to CA(H1), and CA to source N and
  carbonyl C/O backbone. Require complete actual substituents, source charge/H/
  aromaticity and explicit NONE, not element-only assignment or inferred valence.
- Phe neutral six-carbon aromatic sidechain ring CG/CD1/CE1/CZ/CE2/CD2, each actual
  ring edge and full source neighbor/H state; CG attached to CB(H2), CB to CA(H1)
  and source N/carbonyl C/O backbone. Exact formal source graph states, no inferred
  stereochemistry. Existing aromatic and thioether reports remain unchanged.
- Restrict to explicitly N-acylated and C-amidated backbone contexts with source N single to
  CA and the previous carbonyl C and known H1; own carbonyl C single to CA and
  following N, double to O, H0; neutral N/C/O and complete source connectivity.
  This deliberately excludes free termini, unknown links and modified selected-residue contexts.
- Require all ring C isotope masses equal and source isotope states identical;
  thus existing centroid equals ring center of mass without a new mass model.
  Mixed-isotope rings remain unsupported. Do not infer missing isotope data.
- No explicit-H vertices in this initial source-H profile; complete authoritative
  H counts and existing H-consistency checks required. No hydrogen placement.
- Exclude sulfur oxidation/charge/extra substituents, thiol/thiolate/disulfide,
  methionine sulfoxide, sulfonium, Tyr/Trp/heteroaromatics, fused rings, substituted
  Phe sidechains, nonordinary connections, repeated or cross-state ring members.

Geometry/reuse: selected S plus exact six ring members; existing POINT_PLANE can
provide point-to-centroid distance and acute angle under its qualified unique-plane
coverage. Distance proposition does not derive favorability from ring normal.
Degenerate/nonfinite/unqualified source coordinates remain unknown, not negative.
Existing group/correspondence/source-scope/evidence/current gate are sufficient;
no new matcher, ring/path engine, empirical histogram or public schema is needed.

Negatives only for this selected fully eligible pair, with all source chemistry,
identity, complete source connection scope and finite distance current-qualified.
Unknown source state/mapping yields UNKNOWN; known excluded chemistry UNSUPPORTED;
missing independent current authority NOT_EVALUATED. Never whole-system absence.

Acceptance matrix: exact neutral N-acylated/C-amidated Met/Phe positive;7,nextUp7 boundaries; sulfur-only
lookalikes with MET name fail; PHE label with substituted ring fails; sulfide vs
sulfoxide/sulfonium/cysteine; each missing H/charge/NONE/aromaticity/linkage;
unknown residue identity or mapping; mixed ring isotope; same coordinates/different
state; degenerate ring; conflicting source facts and authority; exact duplicate
inputs/reordering; source identity vs graph proof separation; current governed run,
independent JVM, old regressions/65historical/25pins and committed-source run.

This is a bounded source-definition adoption under REQUEST.txt, not production
authority. No public schema/API change or G03 ledger closure is implied.

Normative source-identity and graph-role detail
Select the ordered seven source atom occurrences [MET:SD, PHE:CG, CD1, CE1, CZ,
CE2, CD2]. Each atom must have a unique exact occurrence in the bound state and
one lossless component mapping; MET and PHE must be distinct source residues.
The state snapshot pins source residue name, chain, residue number, insertion code,
atom name and source metadata. Reject duplicate selected names/ambiguous residue
identity rather than choosing by order. Require literal MET/PHE; no alias, modified
residue or sequence-neighbor inference. Source labels and chemical graph proof are
reported separately. Either same or different components/chains are allowed only
within the same immutable state. No biological assembly or alternate-conformer
coexistence inference is made. Exact state provenance remains mandatory.

All bonds below are explicit SINGLE, nonaromatic except the ring and C=O edges.
For each named role require precisely these heavy neighbors, no extras:
MET SD:{CG,CE}, CG:{CB,SD}, CE:{SD}, CB:{CA,CG};
PHE CB:{CA,CG}, CG:{CB,CD1,CD2}, CD1:{CG,CE1}, CE1:{CD1,CZ},
CZ:{CE1,CE2}, CE2:{CZ,CD2}, CD2:{CE2,CG}.
The six ring edges use the existing qualified aromatic-carbon source representation;
Kekule conversion is not performed. The named H counts are MET SD0/CG2/CE3/CB2;
PHE CB2/CG0 and other five ring carbons1. All named sidechain charges are zero;
MET and PHE CB/CA are nonaromatic, and all six PHE ring carbons are aromatic.
For both residues CA is C with neighbors exactly N,CB,C and H1; N is neutral
nonaromatic N with neighbors exactly CA,previousC and H1; C is neutral nonaromatic
C with neighbors exactly CA,O,followingN and H0, double to O; O neutral nonaromatic
O with only C and H0. N/CA/C/O are exact same-residue names. PreviousC must be an
explicit neighboring source carbonyl C in a different residue, with double-O and
ordinary single-N(selected) connectivity; followingN must be an explicit neutral
nonaromatic N in a different residue. These linkage witnesses are matched by actual
bonds, never residue numbering or sequence adjacency. Complete source topology and
source-H consistency are required; a label or the selected local pattern cannot
certify an omitted neighbor. The previous/following residue need not itself be
MET/PHE and is not otherwise given a residue-chemistry classification.

Known extras or incompatible charge/H/bond/aromatic states are UNSUPPORTED for
this restricted pair, not an absent contact. Missing/conflicting required facts
are UNKNOWN. A correctly named pair with no independently established graph roles
never reaches geometry classification. No L/D or absolute stereochemistry inference:
this geometric survey contract preserves recorded stereo without classifying it.

Exact equality of all six source ring isotope descriptors is required (all natural
carbon descriptors or one identical explicit carbon isotope). A missing descriptor
that the source representation cannot distinguish from natural carbon cannot be
upgraded to measured isotope composition. This is the equal-mass source-representation
convention, not an isotope measurement. Mixed explicit descriptors are unsupported.

Reuse is mandatory: existing immutable state/correspondence; existing source-group
and source-H consistency machinery; independent S1 source-scope/current Gate;
unchanged continuous POINT_PLANE operation for centroid distance/raw angle. New
code, if needed, is one G03-specific chemical-role/pair predicate with declarative
source patterns. No new matcher, graph, ring, generic gate or path infrastructure.
The mathematical plane eligibility is inherited from existing geometry qualification;
no scientific planarity tolerance is introduced.

The preceding/following witnesses establish the stated finite amide-linked context
only, not a classification of the entire polymer or biological peptide sequence.
No recursive residue identity is inferred. The full selected MET/PHE source graph
patterns above are the chemical admission definition; other source residue labels
remain attributed labels.

Geometry outcome clarification: finite strictly positive point-to-centroid distance
and qualified nondegenerate ring-plane geometry are eligibility requirements.
A zero point-to-centroid vector is UNKNOWN because its directional geometry is
degenerate, not an ABSENT_FALSE contact. For eligible pairs, <=7A is present and
>7A is selected-pair absent under this exact convention. The source survey does
not specify floating-point equality policy; inclusive7A is explicitly adopted here,
not asserted as byte-level reproduction of its unavailable analysis program.
