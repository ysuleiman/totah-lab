I15 COORDINATION GEOMETRY — source/protocol review, 2026-10-07
Dependencies: exact metal/source state, independently typed ligand/water roles,
complete applicable coordination sphere, correspondence and same-state geometry.
I14 proximity does not provide any of these coordination assignments by itself.
Existing angle/geometry and immutable evidence machinery is sufficient numerically.

Pinned PLIP metal_complexation was re-read end to end. It constructs a candidate
sphere from metal_binding_lig+metal_binding_bs under strict distance<METAL_DIST_MAX
(3.0 in the pinned config), then fits angle-signature heuristics for coordination
numbers2..6. Donor selection is supplied by PLIP preparation, not by the numeric
function. Its score is the mean over targets of sqrt(sum(angle deviations squared)),
not one conventional RMS over the entire sphere. Missing ideal-angle matches are
skipped; e.g. the tetrahedral signature has four109.5 entries per target, while a
four-target sphere has three other target directions. Greedy target/angle assignment,
coordination-count ordering and0.5/3.5 heuristic comparisons choose a result; unused
targets may be excluded. Water-only results are discarded. These are exact source
semantics to preserve if that specific engine protocol is ever adopted; they are
not general chemical definitions of complete coordination geometry.

ProLIF's broader2.8-distance MetalDonor definition is a different pair operation;
it supplies neither a complete sphere nor this PLIP chooser. No combination of the
two can establish a metal coordination geometry without additional source meaning.
The approved source-scope/2 KNOWN_NON_ORDINARY_CONNECTION distinguishes a known
source connection from unknown coverage. It does NOT certify that a complete
coordination sphere has been enumerated, assign a valence contribution, or type
all ligands. Do not expand /2 to those propositions implicitly.

Current exact blocker: no qualified source/preparation protocol selecting complete
I15 coordination spheres and donor/water states for a declared element/oxidation
subdomain; no reviewed policy reconciling the PLIP heuristic's exclusions with
complete-sphere negative coverage. Porting just the angle table would conceal
these missing scientific prerequisites. Universal ideal geometry or an invented
angular tolerance is not proposed.

Disposition stays SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW_REQUIRED, not implemented. A future exact
PLIP-source protocol adoption would require its preparation/type domain and all
heuristics explicitly, or an independent selected-source-sphere contract with
attributable geometry rules. No new generic geometry engine is needed.
Acceptance obligations: exact ideal vs distorted vs competing geometries; missing
ligand/water, competing altloc/state, ambiguous role/mapping, incomplete sphere,
known nonordinary vs unknown source connection, target-order sensitivity, excluded
ligands retained as evidence, water-only semantics, each source boundary, current
independent authority and immutable replay. Expected classifications await the
missing sphere/domain contract; no numerical answers invented.
