2. Source comparison and material choice
Senes et al., PNAS2001,doi10.1073/pnas.161280798/PMC55372, use REDUCE-prepared H,
DSSP helices and a membrane-protein survey. Their Results use a piecewise distance/
angle candidate window; the table footnote states the stricter angular condition.
Ideal-geometry values in their figure are yet another proposition. PLIP strong
hydrogen-bond criteria do not define this weak-donor domain. See METHODS_RECOVERY_REVIEW.
Option A is faithful survey reproduction, still blocked on exact preparation,
helix/acceptor selection and analysis-protocol closure. Option B is this explicit
source-H geometric candidate, limited to a chemically stated glycine subset and
explicitly making no membrane-survey, energetics or general-H-bond parity claim.
Those definitions are materially different. This proposal asks to select B, not
silently combine or relabel it as A.

3. Exact supported chemical/source domain
One explicitly selected ordered tuple [donor CA,one bonded H,acceptor O,acceptor C]
in a single immutable state, with donor and acceptor in distinct complete ordinary
source components. Both source residues are labelled GLY and independently match
the following finite N-acylated/C-amidated glycine source graph. Labels alone are
not chemical evidence. All local named atoms are neutral, nonaromatic and explicit
NONE. CA has exactly N and own carbonyl C as heavy neighbors, both SINGLE, plus
exactly two H in total. N has exactly CA and previous carbonyl C as heavy neighbors,
both SINGLE, and H1. Own C has exactly CA,own O and following N as heavy neighbors,
SINGLE/DOUBLE/SINGLE respectively, H0. O has only own C by DOUBLE and H0. The previous
carbonyl C has exactly selected N,previous O and another carbon as heavy neighbors,
SINGLE/DOUBLE/SINGLE, H0; previous O is neutral O,H0 with only that C. Following N
is neutral nonaromatic, only ordinary SINGLE heavy bonds to carbon; heavy degree
1/2/3 requires independently declared total H2/1/0. All facts are checked against
complete source graph/H evidence; no valence or hydrogen inference supplies them.
Previous/following linkage witnesses are in different source residues from GLY.
Selected acceptor O/C must independently match the unchanged CARBONYL_O role.

The donor CA specifically requires two distinct explicitly mapped H occurrences,
each ordinary SINGLE-bonded only to CA, neutral,H isotope1 or unspecified source
hydrogen, H0 on the H atom, no aromaticity/radical and finite source coordinates.
CA has zero additional implicit H. Each H tuple remains separate; the other H
cannot be chosen implicitly. Acceptor CA may use authoritative implicit H facts,
because its H orientation is not evaluated. Initial components contain only C/N/O
and H vertices; no charged/radical/nonordinary state on the defining local context.
No metal, isotope-labelled donor H, proline, other amino acid, sidechain C-H or
nonpeptide carbon donor is admitted. This is one source-graph donor class, not a
new member of the general P03 donor catalog.

4. Geometric contract and source attribution
Require finite positive d=distance(H,O) and a defined angle z=angle(CA,H,O), in
angstrom/degrees, from explicit source atom coordinates only. The candidate is:
  d < 3.5 AND (z > 120 OR (d < 3.0 AND z > 90)).
All boundaries are strict, as the Results selection is written. At d=3.0 the
relaxed angular branch does not apply; at d=3.5 no tuple qualifies. At z=120 a
short d<3.0 tuple can qualify via the relaxed branch. No donor-acceptor-distance
shortcut, ideal-distance bound, acceptor angle, acidity/pKa or bond-strength
threshold is added. Degenerate/nonfinite/unqualified geometry is UNKNOWN.

Geometry kind is EXPLICIT_SOURCE_H, never inferred-H or heavy-atom proxy. Adopt
the already-qualified water-family attribution convention: supplied prepared H
coordinates remain source-attributed model coordinates and are never relabelled
experimentally observed. Exact source artifacts, preparation/model provenance,
coordinate frame, component/atom mapping and state are retained and independently
reviewed. Athena neither adds H nor reconstructs orientation. Missing required
source/preparation attribution cannot be repaired by assuming REDUCE or any toolkit.
No claim that this reproduces the original membrane-helix preparation/selection.
The original survey's medium-specific energy/stability conclusions are not applied.

6. Acceptance matrix required after approval
- Positive glycine tuple in each strict-window branch; two distinct donor H tuples
  remain separate even with shared CA/acceptor. Eligible negative at long distance
  or inappropriate direction, not donor-O distance alone.
- d=3.0 and d=3.5 with nextDown/nextUp; z=90 and z=120 with nextDown/nextUp;
  short-distance z=120 differs from the table-only definition.
- Same geometry with non-GLY labels, alpha-carbon sidechain substitution, amide
  linkage change, protonated N, nonneutral carbonyl, radical or nonordinary source;
  glycine-like name is insufficient. Distinct-component restriction enforced.
- Missing one donor H, nonzero implicit donor H, wrong H connectivity, unresolved
  H/charge/aromaticity/NONE, incomplete mapping/graph, missing preparation source,
  conflicting evidence and implicit consumption of historical source.
- Supplied modeled H retains its provenance and is not called observed; I03 proxy
  or inferred H cannot be substituted. Different state with identical coordinates
  cannot replay. Original atoms/order/source evidence remain immutable.
- Exact source/definition/role/measurement pins; changed bytes and expired or absent
  authority; current governed execution; independent JVM; affected regressions,
  historical65/pins25 and fresh committed source before any bounded ledger closure.
