2. PRIMARY SOURCE / DEFINITIONS COMPARED
Imai et al., Protein Science17(2008)1129–1137, DOI10.1110/ps.033910.107,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2442010/.
Pinned HTML/text and Figure1 are in bounded-literature-review-20261008/reference;
Figure1 was inspected directly again in this review.
The paper's geometric screen is Cl-centroid distance<4.5 A and theta<140 degrees.
Figure1 places theta at the nearest ring atom R: angle(Cl,R,ring centroid).
It is NOT C-Cl-centroid, not a ring-normal angle, and not an inferred hydrogen angle.
The paper additionally removed CH-Cl cases by final visual inspection. Its final
59-record survey, energetic model calculations and biological discussion are NOT
reproduced or adopted as this predicate's truth. This is a geometric candidate.
The survey includes aromatic and two nonaromatic carbon-bound chlorine ligands;
Phe is one explicitly studied protein aromatic side chain. Narrowing to Phe avoids
inventing Tyr/Trp/His ring-center, tautomer or competing Cl-N conventions.
I10 atom-acceptor halogen definitions and their cutoffs are distinct. RING's general
halogen-pi description did not supply the executable core criterion in the earlier
local review. Neither a shared name nor generic PLIP/ProLIF thresholds overrides
this exact source definition. HalBS distributions/scores remain a separate,
unselected reference-statistics/protocol problem. No F/Br/I transfer is proposed.

3. EXACT PROPOSED CHEMICAL / STRUCTURAL DOMAIN
Selected tuple: [bound carbon,Cl,CG,CD1,CE1,CZ,CE2,CD2]. All atoms are distinct,
source-bound, in one immutable qualified coordinate state. The Cl and PHE source
components are distinct, complete, connected and have unique, lossless, nonoverlapping
source correspondences. Selected Cl is neutral, closed-shell, H0, with exactly one
ordinary single covalent bond to the selected neutral carbon; no nonordinary source
connection is permitted. Carbon may be source-aromatic or source-aliphatic; no
hybridization, bond energy or favorable-interaction inference is made.
Use existing explicit source charge/H/aromaticity facts and independent source-scope
review. Missing facts or contradictory evidence remain unknown. Source preparation
and any modeled coordinates retain their actual provenance.
PHE is the exact unchanged G03 finite neutral N-acylated/C-amidated source context:
unique source PHE label and N/CA/C/O/CB/CG/CD1/CE1/CZ/CE2/CD2 correspondences; neutral
source CA(H1)-N(H1)/carbonyl/CB(H2) backbone context; CB-CG attachment; the six source
aromatic carbons and literal AROMATIC ring bonds with CG H0 and other ring members
H1; the same previous-carbonyl/following-amide guards. Use the unchanged aromatic
pattern and the same uniform ring-isotope descriptor restriction. Do not broaden
G03 or its role manifest merely to reuse this local context.
These source chemical facts do NOT by themselves prove that a selected component
is a ligand or a protein. That is the separate boundary in section8.

4. EXACT GEOMETRY / CHARACTERIZATION
G is the existing unweighted centroid of the complete six-member source PHE ring.
Measure all six d(Cl,R_i) through existing DISTANCE operations. A unique minimum
selects R; exact tied minima are inconclusive, with every tied identity retained.
No input-order tie-break or invented near-tie tolerance.
A supplied POINT_PAIR_GROUP operation [Cl,R] against the same complete ring must
bind that actual unique nearest R. Its first-centroid distance is d(Cl,G); its
first-second-centroid angle is theta=angle(Cl,R,G). A declared wrong nearest atom
is a selection/provenance failure, never a convenient alternative angle.
Candidate iff d(Cl,G)<4.5 A AND theta<140 degrees, with both boundaries strict.
The selected-pair negative is allowed only after full eligibility/coverage and a
failed predicate. Finite positive distances, nondegenerate required vectors,
qualified common frame and the existing numerical ring-normal uniqueness guard
are required; no empirical planarity, strain or angular tolerance is invented.
Characterization only: delta=abs(d(Cl,G)-d(Cl,R)); delta<=0.3 A is source-defined
face-on, otherwise edge-on. It neither changes the candidate predicate nor proves
energy/affinity. Retain actual six distances, nearest identity/ties, centroid,
theta, delta and available orientation diagnostics independently.

8. PRECISE REMAINING REPRESENTATION / AUTHORITY GAP
SystemStateView.Component has identity, graph, correspondence and limitations, but
no independently bound protein/ligand attribution. Its snapshot preserves Gaia
ResidueClassificationEvidence(classification,source,reason). Those fields retain
labels; they do not carry a qualified partner-role protocol or source witness pins.
STANDARD_AMINO_ACID identifies a chemical category, not protein membership.
POLYMER includes nonprotein polymers. HETERO/HETATM does not prove ligand identity.
Gaia's PolymerType.PROTEIN enum exists but is not carried by Component/Chain or a
qualified partner-attribution source record in this foundation path. Hermes's
current PdbReader yields CCD-derived STANDARD_AMINO_ACID/POLYMER/HETERO categories;
it does not independently establish the exact two roles required here.
The current SP3_SOURCE_SCOPE/1 and /2 assertions establish connection/electronic
coverage. They MUST NOT be reinterpreted as protein/ligand classification authority.
The earlier literature review's 'no public schema currently required' conclusion
was correct for the geometric operations but incomplete for partner admission.
This source audit supersedes that part only; it does not change any qualified rule.

SMALLEST PROPOSED ADDITION — requires approval before implementation
ATHENA.I11.SOURCE_PARTNER_ATTRIBUTION/1, a normative measurement contract carried
by the EXISTING EvidenceInterpretation serialization. No SystemStateView API change
and no new top-level envelope/schema or generic classification framework.
Exact measurement keys, all strings as required by EvidenceInterpretation:
- proposition: literal ATHENA.I11.SOURCE_PARTNER_ATTRIBUTION/1;
- stateBinding: canonical existing SystemStateView.Binding JSON;
- ligandComponentReference: canonical existing component ScientificReference JSON;
- proteinComponentReference: canonical existing component ScientificReference JSON;
- ligandAtoms: canonical sorted array of existing AtomReference objects, exhaustive
  for the explicitly selected ligand component's bound source correspondence;
- proteinAtoms: the same for the explicitly selected protein component;
- stateSnapshotPin: canonical existing {reference,sha256} envelope pin to the exact
  preserved source state snapshot;
- sourceProtocol: canonical existing {reference,sha256} envelope pin to the exact
  attributed source partner-assignment/selection protocol;
- originalSourcePins: canonical sorted nonempty array of existing envelope pins
  for the original evidence establishing these partner assignments.
No unknown fields, duplicate pins, overlapping/partial atom sets or implicit source
lookup. Distinct components and exact state/selection/protocol/original-byte identity
are mandatory. Interpretation inputs must equal the union of the snapshot, protocol
and original-source pins; its evaluator must equal the enclosing envelope method.
SUPPORTED_PRESENT means only the two declared partner assignments are explicitly
supported by the selected original source and protocol. UNKNOWN_INCONCLUSIVE retains
unresolved/conflicting attribution; UNSUPPORTED retains a known outside-domain
assignment with its original evidence. ABSENT_FALSE is not an allowed attribution
status. These statuses never establish Cl/PHE chemistry, geometry or interactions.
A supplied source assertion cannot self-qualify. Admission additionally requires
independent CURRENT review of BOTH the exact interpretation bytes (including actual
producer identity and all source pins) and its protocol using the existing Research
Gate, at the same evaluation time and state. Historical/inherited or expired review
cannot authorize the current tuple. Missing authority remains NOT_EVALUATED.
No automatic reader, CCD lookup, name/HETATM heuristic or production protocol is
selected by this proposal. A future real producer/protocol and original source
witnesses must be attributed and independently qualified. Engineering fixtures are
explicitly synthetic; they do not supply production source authority.

Decision: do not implement I11 yet. Geometry is supportable; silently inventing
partner admission would broaden the cited domain. Approve this exact minimal public
evidence clarification before implementing the bounded I11 leaf and its qualification.
