report a caller-selected angle/distance, so a new general geometry engine adds
nothing and cannot close the scientific remainder.

invented here. Full SAM versus SAH and generic sulfonium remain different identities;
source-labelled methyl, inferred hydrogens and favorable reaction semantics remain
separate. Raw descriptors stay available without upgrading them to catalysis.
