PROPOSED EXACT PROPOSITION
An occurrence is the existing two-carbon triple-bond membership, with at least
one explicitly attributed terminal-carbon role. A terminal-carbon role requires:
- supplied neutral nonaromatic carbon;
- source triple bond to supplied neutral nonaromatic carbon;
- exactly one non-hydrogen neighbor;
- authoritative total one-H state (explicit plus supplied implicit count),
  consistent with source chemistry and existing B00/coverage semantics.
The two carbon members and all correspondence alternatives are preserved.
Explicit H remains an actual source atom in the context; implicit H never becomes
an invented atom/coordinate. Unknown H cannot establish the identity or its absence.
