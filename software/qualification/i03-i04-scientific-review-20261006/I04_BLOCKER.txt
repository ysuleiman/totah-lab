I04 WEAK C-H DONOR INTERACTIONS — SCIENTIFIC BLOCKER / NO IMPLEMENTATION

Finding: no complete attributable bounded classification contract has been selected
from the evidence actually inspected in this phase. Stop I04 implementation here.
This is not evidence that C-H...O interactions do not exist, nor a permanent
unsupported disposition. Keep I04 SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW_REQUIRED and count unchanged.

Candidate subfamilies are NOT interchangeable:
1. PLIP hydrophobic-carbon enumeration: C with ONLY C/H direct neighbors, explicit
   attached H, independent OpenBabel regular acceptors. No acidity restriction is
   present. The pinned weak detection path is not implemented; its strong-only
   4.1 A/100-degree comparisons cannot authorize a weak C-H classifier. This broad
   enumeration is rejected for I04. In particular an ordinary methyl C-H does
   not acquire donor eligibility by being hydrophobic or geometrically close.
2. Protein alpha-peptide C-alpha-H...O: primary protein studies support an exact
   context worth reviewing, distinct from methyl or aryl C-H. Backbone attachment
   to N and carbonyl carbon would have to be graph-backed, not inferred from CA
   atom name; Gly two-H and other residues' one-H cases need separate handling.
   Neutral backbone amide carbonyl O is a plausible restricted acceptor; the exact
   source population (e.g. membrane helix interfaces), exclusion rules, prepared-H
   protocol, distances, angular definitions and boundary operators must first be
   retrieved and adopted. Those full methods were NOT verified here. No numeric
   pKa/acidity range, helix threshold or geometric cutoff is proposed.
3. Terminal alkynyl C(sp)-H...O/N: existing F18 neutral source terminal-alkyne
   identity can locate a motif; it is not an acidity or interaction qualification.
   The primary acetylene literature identified is system-specific, including
   gas-phase water/ammonia complexes. It cannot be generalized to arbitrary
   organic oxygen/nitrogen acceptors, substituted alkynes, solvent or proteins
   by copying an equilibrium bond length as a cutoff. No such transfer is proposed.
4. Aryl/heteroaryl, aldehydic, halo-substituted, cation-adjacent or other activated
   C-H: neither pooled into one weak-donor class nor admitted via a qualitative
   'acidic C-H' label. Substitution, charge, resonance and acceptor context would
   each require an attributed definition and separate source applicability review.

Exact approved I04 donor classes: NONE. Exact approved I04 acceptor pairings: NONE.
Existing carbonyl/amine roles can establish independent acceptor identities only;
they cannot provide missing C-H donor or class-pair qualification. A proposed
future C-alpha/backbone-carbonyl pair is a research scope, not an approved rule.

Acidity/hybridization: no pKa cutoff, electronegativity score, sp/sp2/sp3 acidity
ranking or inferred activation window is introduced. A source-assigned sp label
is a descriptor, not proof of a C-H donor. Motif membership and acidity evidence
must remain separate. Missing pKa cannot be replaced by a guessed class mean.
No dataset, empirical distribution or new external-data ledger row is fabricated.

Quantities available for a future explicit-source-H contract:
C-A and H-A distances, angle(C,H,A), and any source-specified acceptor antecedent
angles/planes, all with raw values and source atom references. These are measurable
using existing geometry. Numerical membership limits are UNSET, as are the exact
class-specific required quantities; therefore no geometry-only I04 positive or
negative can be emitted. Measurement-only C/H/A export would not close I04 and
is not authorized as a substitute implementation in this phase.

Provenance: exact source C-H bond, complete state H/charge/topology and mapping,
explicit attribution of observed versus prepared H, acceptor class/context,
preparation and domain/protocol versions, immutable coordinates. Inferred-H I04
would require a separately approved placement model (I03-A supplies none).
No combining C/H/A from different states or minimizing H direction toward A.

Negative coverage: cannot be fully defined until the donor/acceptor class universe
and applicable geometry/preparation protocol are selected. Eventually require
complete graph-backed candidate inventory, possible unresolved donors retained,
full class/context coverage, every required H/geometry/acceptor observation and
exhaustive scoped enumeration, plus independent bound inventory coverage. A
hypothetical threshold failure alone does not support absence today. Known
excluded classes stay outside that profile, not negatives for all weak bonding.

EXACT REOPEN CONDITIONS

- Obtain and pin primary methods or executable protocol including all geometry
  definitions, boundaries, H treatment, donor/acceptor restrictions and exclusions.
- Decide whether a source-defined operational contact candidate, physical H-bond
  evidence, or an energetic assertion is intended; do not interchange them.
- Justify any transfer from the source population/model to our bounded domain.
- Review the resulting complete scientific definition and class-pair acceptance
  matrix before adding a weak-donor role or predicate. A public schema/API change
  requires its own approval. Existing matcher/geometry/event/gate tools suffice
  for many such choices; no new generic primitive is justified by this blocker.

Acceptance matrix includes prospective positive-admission requirements, negative,
ambiguous/missing/conflicting source cases and forbidden cross-definition replay.
No synthetic passing I04 interaction is invented to manufacture completion.
