SOURCE COMPARISON (revision-specific)
S1 ProLIF interactions.py, commit19f1800218387c49536eb9d3e8cd3044fdb337ee:
HBAcceptor uses an explicit matched H, D-A<=3.5 A and DHA in [130,180] degrees.
ImplicitHBAcceptor instead measures heavy-neighbor-centered angles; it creates
no H coordinate and no DHA angle. Distance<=3.5 A. Ideal angles come from the
source hybridization table: SP=180, SP2=120, SP3=109.5 degrees. For each center,
minimum absolute deviation over heavy-neighbor angles is used, NOT maximum,
not an averaged direction, and not a unique inferred H vector. Donor tolerance
25 degrees; acceptor tolerance90. For SP2, out-of-plane donor<=30 degrees and
acceptor<=90. Boundaries are inclusive (rejection uses >); the base Distance
class uses <=. Water identification is residue-name based in this source and
optional water behavior can bypass one/both geometry sides. This is NOT the
qualified I12/I13 water identity and will not be imported.

Source chemistry is broader/different: implicit donor SMARTS includes S and
charged N contexts; acceptor includes selected F and exclusion patterns. The
explicit and implicit source acceptor expressions themselves differ. Pin their
literal source bytes, do not summarize them into a universal donor/acceptor rule.
The full implicit implementation appends a radius-dependent Vina-like metadata
term with endpoints -0.425 and0.565 A using CSD radii; its code/comment terminology
does not establish a calibrated probability. It is not part of I03-A membership,
will not be imported, and no radii table/dataset is selected by this review.
Its SP2 plane helper selects the first two heavy neighbors, extending to second
neighbors when needed: source ordering and insufficient/collinear neighbors need
an explicit policy before any general SP2 contract. A best-fit plane substitution
would be a different algorithm, not harmless reuse.

S2 PLIP commit2f4911d307490479ac023b22d6faa8f59b577ca8:
find_hbd enumerates regular donors and weak pairs from carbon atoms whose direct
neighbors are exclusively C/H. This does not restrict donors by acidity and can
include ordinary hydrocarbon C-H while excluding C-H adjacent directly to N/O.
The pinned hbonds implementation immediately skips typ != 'strong'; normal calls
use get_hbd and 'strong'. Thus this source does NOT supply an executed weak-bond
classifier or a validated C-H threshold. Strong-path comparisons are strictly
0.5 < D-A <4.1 A and DHA>100 degrees. Despite the docstring mentioning H distance,
the membership comparison uses D-A; H-A is recorded. These strong-path values
must not be repurposed for I04 or merged with ProLIF's inclusive limits.
