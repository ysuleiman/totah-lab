DOMAINS AND LIMITATIONS
All definitions require attributed complete graph and formal-charge coverage.
Component elements are bounded to H/C/N/O/S. No normalization of chemical state.
CARBONYL: literal neutral trigonal C=O identity. Charged/resonance alternatives
outside this representation unsupported. A literal C=O within a carboxylate
may retain its identity, without treating the alternate oxygen as equivalent.
ESTER: carbon-substituted neutral acyl C(=O)-O-C subtype. Formate unsupported;
acid, carbonate and carbamate are negatives only for this bounded subtype.
AMIDE: carbon-substituted neutral acyl C(=O)-N subtype. Formamide unsupported;
charged N and charge-separated forms unsupported. Carbonyl overlap retained.
AROMATIC: isolated six-carbon neutral aromatic cycle under OCL/2026.7.2 source
model. Kekule/aromatic fixture representations qualified; heteroaromatic,
fused/uncovered aromatic environments unsupported. No planarity inference.
METHYL: neutral carbon, authoritative three H and one non-H attachment.
THIOL: neutral carbon-attached S, authoritative one H and one heavy attachment.
THIOLATE: explicit -1 S, authoritative zero H and one carbon attachment.
For H-dependent definitions, relevant authoritative H counts 0..3 are checked
against B00 consistency queries; counts outside that adapter corpus unsupported.
Unknown H never supplied from OCL inference. Source zero annotation stays
unspecified. Explicit graph H, authoritative implicit H, source annotations and
B00 receipts remain separately recorded. Incomplete relevant H coverage prevents
negative conclusions, while an independently covered positive occurrence can
survive unknown state elsewhere. No protonation, pKa or reactivity conclusion.
