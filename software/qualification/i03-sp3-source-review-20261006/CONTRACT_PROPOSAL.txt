PROPOSED SCIENTIFIC CONTRACT — NOT APPROVED / NOT IMPLEMENTED
ATHENA.I03.N_SP3.SATURATED_CARBON_ATTACHMENTS/1 (S1)
Date: 2026-10-06; qualified implementation baseline622045b38.

1. PROPOSITION AND LIMIT
Assign the explicit model label SP3 to a source nitrogen only when it satisfies
this bounded neutral, closed-shell, three-sigma-bond amine graph contract. This
is an Athena Lewis/valence model assignment for I03-A applicability, not a measured
orbital decomposition, proof of tetrahedral geometry, unique lone-pair direction,
H orientation, basicity, donor strength or favorable interaction. The integer
valence conditions below define the reviewed model; they are not an unreviewed
fallback heuristic. A formal lone pair follows within this closed-shell neutral
three-bond N model; no general electron-configuration algorithm is proposed.

This producer is a CONSERVATIVE SUBSET of the existing I03-A input domain, not
coverage of every nitrogen that could ever be called SP3. No universal atom
hybridization or SP/SP2 classifier. Non-SP3 outputs below are bounded diagnostics.
No new donor/acceptor role is introduced. Six existing ordered interaction class
pairs remain unchanged; production activation is NOT granted by this proposal.

2. EXACT CLASSES AND PATTERNS
The only admission roles (unchanged full manifests in EXACT_ROLE_REUSE.json):
ATHENA.PERCEPTION.DONOR.AMINE_PRIMARY
ATHENA.PERCEPTION.DONOR.AMINE_SECONDARY
ATHENA.PERCEPTION.ACCEPTOR.AMINE_PRIMARY
ATHENA.PERCEPTION.ACCEPTOR.AMINE_SECONDARY
ATHENA.PERCEPTION.ACCEPTOR.AMINE_TERTIARY
The chemical patterns are respectively N(C)H2, N(C)2H1, N(C)3H0; all N-C/N-H
edges SINGLE, N nonaromatic, formal charge0, source electronic token NONE.
The same primary/secondary N can have separately attributed donor and acceptor
roles. The assignment is atom-level and role-independent once these admissible
role witnesses are supplied; it does not merge the roles or ordered I03 pairs.
Tertiary amine is acceptor-only in I03-A; ammonia has no admitted class.

Let Cn be the complete set of direct non-H neighbors of candidate n, k=|Cn|,
and h(n)=count(distinct explicit source H bonded to n)+authoritative implicitH(n).
SP3 requires exactly one of (k,h(n))=(1,2),(2,1),(3,0); k+h(n)=3;
all direct heavy neighbors are carbon and all incident bonds are SINGLE;
no aromatic/delocalized incident bond; known nonaromatic N and each Cn;
N formal charge0 and complete, consistent authoritative H state.
N may carry explicit or authoritative implicit H for assignment through its
acceptor role; mixed inventories must be fully attributed and consistent. This
does not relax I03-A donor eligibility: donor N must independently have zero
explicit H and authoritative implicitH2/1. An SP3 assignment never deletes H or
qualifies a donor role by itself.
Explicit H occurrences are neutral, nonaromatic, degree1 with a SINGLE bond,
no implicit H of their own, and independently supplied electronic token NONE.
Do not count Atom.explicitHydrogens a second time. Existing source-coverage
explicitHydrogenAtomIds and implicitHydrogenCount are the authoritative inventory.

For EACH carbon c in Cn (the additional conservative saturation guard):
formal charge0; source electronic token NONE; known nonaromatic;
complete list of ALL incident bonds, every one SINGLE and nonaromatic;
number of explicit neighbor occurrences + authoritative implicitH(c) =4.
Explicit bound H has the same consistency conditions above. No carbon
hybridization label is requested or inferred. This is a finite local bond/count
predicate. N plus its carbon attachments and every edge incident on those carbons
is the decision neighborhood; complete component topology supplies it.
No selected convenient subset of a carbon's incident bonds is permitted.

Every component atom must have authoritative source identity/formal charge,
source H counts consistent with graph, known aromaticity under the existing pinned
source protocol, and explicit electronic-state coverage as described below.
Supported component elements remain H,C,N,O,S,F,Cl,Br,I. Remote ordinary carbonyl,
aromatic, or charged groups do not automatically disqualify a candidate provided
all facts are covered and the direct carbon attachments satisfy the guard.
The proposal allows saturated cyclic/bridged N environments satisfying the same
pattern, including aziridine and quinuclidine. This is a formal model assignment;
no claim that their bond angles equal109.5 degrees or that strain is negligible.
An acyclic-only alternative is separately surfaced for approval in REVIEW.txt.

3. REQUIRED INDEPENDENT SOURCE FACTS
- Exact immutable SystemStateView binding, component graph and source artifact
  pins; unique total injective atom correspondence; source and system topology
  agree. Preserve atom identities/order; no normalizing, protonating, tautomerizing,
  deleting H, adding H or sanitizing the source into eligibility.
- Complete authoritative charges, H inventories/modes, aromaticity evidence and
  exact ordinary bond orders. A toolkit default, SMARTS role name, default false
  aromatic flag or missing H annotation is not a source fact.
- Explicit V03 electronic-state token athena.ocl.atomRadicalState/1=NONE for
  EVERY atom of the selected component, including represented explicit H, with
  pinned source/preparation provenance. This is an existing representation token,
  not a call to OCL to produce electronic state. Absence of the property is UNKNOWN;
  zero returned by an unqualified toolkit is not an assertion of NONE.
  Any explicit S/D/T token in the component is outside S1. This whole-component
  closed-shell restriction is conservative and requires approval. It is a formal
  supplied electronic-state model, not proof of the molecule's quantum ground state.
- Lossless source atom/connection scope: a separately attributed source-scope
  interpretation must establish that the selected component and its incident
  connections are completely represented as ordinary covalent connections with
  no omitted/represented coordinate, dative, zero-order or metal connection.
  This is a represented-source proposition, not proof that physical coordination
  is impossible. MolecularGraph has no DATIVE bond type: an adapter that discards
  such input cannot qualify this assertion. EXPLICIT connectivity alone is not
  sufficient proof of lossless coordination coverage. See EVIDENCE_PROPOSAL.txt.
- Replay the unchanged exact role manifests and their occurrence-local exclusions.
  A role match alone proves neither closed shell nor the new saturation guard.
Source-scope coverage proves coverage only; it cannot assign charge, H state,
aromaticity, NONE, bond order or SP3, and cannot repair a missing property.

4. RESONANCE / CHEMICAL EXCLUSIONS
Preserve exact existing [N]-[C](=[O,N,S]) and [N]-[S](=[O]) exclusions with anchored
witnesses. They cover the existing acyl/amidino and sulfonyl contexts; do not
replace them with a toolkit class label or enlarge their SMARTS silently.
Additional S1 guard excludes EVERY directly attached carbon having a multiple or
aromatic incident bond, known nonzero charge, or non-four-coordinate ordinary
source pattern. This covers N-C=C (enamine), N-C#C (ynamine), N-C=O/N/S,
N-aromatic-C and analogous source unsaturation without a generic resonance engine.
Direct attachment to non-carbon heavy atoms is outside S1, including N-S/O/N/P/B
or a metal. Explicit unsupported radical/electronic or coordination context is
outside S1. Unknown versions/facts are UNKNOWN, not negative.
No remote resonance search, steric rescue, ring-size threshold, pKa/acidity rule,
partial-charge cutoff, or geometry-based exception. Benzyl/allyl/alpha-carbonyl
amines with a saturated carbon spacer are not confused with direct attachment
of N to an aromatic/alkenic/carbonyl carbon. All other conditions still apply.

Class treatment:
Amides/carbamates/ureas/thioamides: existing anchored exclusion; OUT_OF_DOMAIN,
including twisted/bridgehead variants; no blanket physical SP2 assertion.
Amidines/guanidines: amino N excluded by context; imino N has direct N multiple
bond (bounded NOT_SP3 diagnostic when applicable); no tautomer/protonation choice.
Sulfonamides: non-carbon neighbor and existing sulfonyl exclusion; OUT_OF_DOMAIN.
Aniline-like attachment: OUT_OF_DOMAIN even with ortho substitution or a constrained
pyramidal N. No steric rescue. Toolkit disagreement cannot enlarge this profile.
Charged N (ammonium, quaternary N, anions, N-oxides): OUT_OF_DOMAIN, NOT blanket
NOT_SP3. Quaternary ammonium may receive SP3 in another model but has no S1 role.
Radicals/open-shell: any known S/D/T in component OUT_OF_DOMAIN; missing/contradictory
NONE evidence UNKNOWN. Do not infer electronic state from ordinary valence.
Metals/coordination: known incident coordinate/dative/metal connection or metal
in the selected component OUT_OF_DOMAIN; unknown/lossy connection scope UNKNOWN.
Unrelated metal in a separately represented component does not itself prove a
connection; its absence from the covalent graph does not prove noncoordination.
Unusual valence/query atoms/ambiguous bonds: OUT_OF_DOMAIN if conclusively outside
ordinary supported patterns, UNKNOWN if facts conflict or remain unresolved.
Nitrogen isotopes use the same element/count model; isotope/source identity is
preserved. Deuterium/tritium count as H only via authoritative element/isotope
identity; no isotope geometry or energetic inference.

5. DECISIONS AND PRECEDENCE
Integrity failure (changed bytes, identity collision, malformed reference/schema,
foreign state) rejects the invocation, preserving original evidence.
Conflicting material source facts -> UNKNOWN_INCONCLUSIVE; preserve every witness.
A conclusively known exclusion can exclude the model without inventing missing
facts, but never override a contradiction concerning that exclusion's witness.
With complete internally consistent applicable source facts:
SP3: all S1 clauses and at least one exact admitted role satisfied.
NOT_SP3: only a neutral closed-shell source N with a known directly incident
DOUBLE/TRIPLE bond or known aromatic N; report the exact witness. This means
incompatible with S1's nonaromatic three-single-bond model, not an implemented
SP2/SP determination, spectroscopy result or interaction-negative. Such boundary
atoms cannot be admitted through the five positive roles.
UNSUPPORTED/OUT_OF_DOMAIN: other known excluded classes, resonance contexts,
charge, radical, coordination, element or unusual-valence cases. These must not
be collapsed into NOT_SP3 or silently dropped as qualified negative chemistry.
UNKNOWN: any unresolved required admission/exclusion fact, missing coverage,
ambiguous correspondence, inconsistent role/source evidence or unqualified input
provenance. No default SP3, default neutral, default NONE or convenient resonance form.
A returned SP3 model label remains scientifically ineligible until producer
implementation, definition, input model and current invocation are independently
qualified. Approval of this document alone would not issue authority.

6. GEOMETRY
NONE. No coordinates, fitted plane, pyramidalization, angle sum, distance,
inversion barrier or orbital calculation contributes to assignment. Changing

preparation remain bound. I03-A later uses its unchanged real-heavy-atom numeric
predicate; neither that109.5-degree reference nor a passing proxy proves SP3.
No hydrogen position is created or claimed to be observed.

7. NEGATIVE COVERAGE / I03 COMPOSITION
Enumerate every explicitly selected N; record SP3/NOT_SP3/OUT_OF_DOMAIN/UNKNOWN
with provenance. Missing classes/evidence never yield an empty covered negative.
No state-wide or physical non-hydrogen-bond conclusion. Completeness is relative
to the explicit selection AND this S1 applicability contract. A result from a
strict subset cannot be advertised as exhaustive for alternative SP3 models.
I03 negative coverage additionally requires independent inventory exhaustiveness,
complete unchanged role/source chemistry, both endpoints qualified under S1,
all neighbor geometry and enumeration coverage, and current scientific authority.
NOT_SP3 or OUT_OF_DOMAIN is not an ABSENT_FALSE hydrogen bond. UNKNOWN at a
potentially eligible selected N blocks complete scientific negative coverage.
An incomplete positive inventory cannot certify counts. Inventory/source-scope
coverage and producer qualification remain independent propositions.
Future composition must explicitly bind S1 protocol/qualification to I03-A scope;
no reuse of the old full-manifest digest/receipt after activation applicability
changes. Do not alter historical I03, I02, water or event/path interpretations.
