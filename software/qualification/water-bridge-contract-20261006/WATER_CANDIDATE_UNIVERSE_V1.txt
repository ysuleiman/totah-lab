APPROVED-CONTRACT CLARIFICATION — ATHENA.WATER.CANDIDATE_UNIVERSE/1
2026-10-06. Normative before implementation; chemistry is not broadened.

Universe: every oxygen occurrence in the exact selected source SystemStateView,
not only HOH-labelled residues or query matches, and not a distance-cut pocket.
An oxygen occurrence is addressed by its existing AtomReference and complete
state binding. Inspect authoritative component chemistry via source correspondence
and replay-verified source coverage. Structural O without resolved chemistry or
correspondence remains in the universe as unresolved. No O atom is invented.

Possibly-water O = an O occurrence that satisfies the exact neutral-water
definition OR cannot be conclusively excluded solely because required identity
facts are unresolved. The following classification is exhaustive:
  QUALIFIED_WATER: exact neutral-water identity proved; candidate. H orientation
    is a separate assessment and can still be unavailable for implicit-H water.
  UNRESOLVED_WATER_ID: no conclusive exclusion and identity not fully proved;
    candidate and blocks complete negative coverage.
  DEFINITELY_NOT_WATER: at least one conclusive exclusion below, supported by
    nonconflicting authoritative source facts; does not block water coverage.
These are water-identity diagnostics, not new EvidenceInterpretation statuses.

Conclusive exclusions (each sufficient; no other exclusion is authorized):
E1. Known nonzero formal charge on O.
E2. Known aromatic O under the supplied qualified aromaticity model.
E3. A known covalent bond from O to any non-H atom, including O-O peroxide and
    O-metal connectivity. Distance to a metal is not a covalent bond.
E4. At least three distinct known source H atoms bonded to O, or a complete,
    authoritative total source H count different from exactly two. Fewer than
    two explicit H with unresolved implicit H is NOT sufficient exclusion.
E5. A known O-H bond order other than SINGLE.
E6. A known O-bound H with nonzero formal charge, or a known additional covalent
    neighbor of that H other than the selected O.

Known means bound, unambiguous, authoritative source evidence. A default graph
charge without SUPPORTED_PRESENT charge coverage is not a known charge.
Source bond assertions must agree between mapped component and structure graphs;
an inferred/unknown connectivity model does not supply conclusive covalent facts.
An authoritative total-H count requires a non-UNKNOWN H mode, nonnegative supplied
implicit count, exact agreement of listed explicit H IDs with source H bonds,
and no source contradiction. Never use OCL-inferred hydrogens as source facts.
Known aromaticity requires SUPPORTED_PRESENT and the pinned OCL/2026.7.2 model.
Unrelated missing facts do not undo a nonconflicting conclusive exclusion (e.g.
an authoritative C-O bond excludes water even if the O H count is unavailable).
Conflicting identity facts/duplicate contradictory source reports are unresolved
or integrity failures, never a selected convenient exclusion. Ambiguous mapping
cannot justify exclusion based on a single preferred correspondence alternative.

Qualified water requires independent replay-verified exact neutral-water group
occurrence, all applicable identity facts, agreement of complete graph/charge/H
state and source mapping, and no exclusion. Orientation eligibility additionally
requires two mapped neutral explicit H atoms, both SINGLE-bonded to this O only,
no implicit H, a qualified coordinate frame and finite nondegenerate O-H vectors.
No water-internal bond length or H-O-H angular window is introduced.

Inventory comparison includes every QUALIFIED_WATER and UNRESOLVED_WATER_ID O.
Omitted members defeat complete negative coverage. Listed DEFINITELY_NOT_WATER
entries remain reported as excluded; they cannot become eligible via listing.
A valid positive witness can survive unrelated unresolved inventory entries.

NORMATIVE SEPARATION OF PROPOSITIONS
inventoryCoverage proves ONLY exhaustiveness of the declared candidate inventory
for the exact bound source state and selection protocol. It establishes NONE of:
water identity, total/explicit H completeness, H orientation, chemical role
eligibility, incident-leg eligibility, scientific support or current qualification.
All those propositions require their own qualified source chemistry/geometry/
research evidence. A complete inventory containing an unresolved O remains
ineligible for a complete negative. A complete inventory containing a non-water
O cannot produce a water leg. Inventory assertions cannot override exclusions,
contradictions, missing H or a failed geometry/Research Gate.

This predicate version and its file digest are pinned by both water manifests.
Any exclusion change is a scientific-definition change requiring review.
