The 12 external-reference-data requirements remain outside this family.

2. PROPOSED DEFINITION / CHOSEN CONTRACT
I12 rule: ATHENA.WATER_BRIDGE.EXPLICIT_H_SINGLE/1.0.0.
I13 rule: ATHENA.WATER_BRIDGE.EXPLICIT_H_MULTI/1.0.0.
Implementation identity proposed: athena.water-bridge/1.
Named proposition: an explicit-source-H water-mediated structural candidate,
not established hydrogen bonding, hydration, energetics or proton transfer.
Every leg is an exact supplied tuple (D,H,A), with an authoritative SINGLE D-H
bond, finite nondegenerate coordinates, D-A <= 3.5 angstrom and angle D-H-A
in [130,180] degrees, vertex H. Preserve D-A, H-A and D-H-A separately.
These are the pinned ProLIF HBAcceptor/HBDonor defaults, selected as an
attributed operational definition, not fitted or invented empirical thresholds.
No lower distance bound, H-A cutoff, inter-leg angle or occupancy threshold is
added. Source structural validity remains separate from this candidate screen.

Water identity is a supplied neutral nonaromatic oxygen with exactly two
source hydrogens, no heavy covalent neighbor, and no additional hydrogen or
covalent edge. Each represented H is neutral and has exactly one SINGLE bond,
to that O. Retain isotope labels; no isotope-dependent energetic assertion.
Chemical identity may be expressible with implicit H, but ALL water H atoms
must be explicit, mapped and coordinate-bearing before ANY incident leg is
eligible, including a leg on which water is only the acceptor. The same
water O and both H coordinates are reused throughout the whole path.
Residue name HOH/WAT and an oxygen position alone are insufficient identity.

Non-water roles reuse the exact pinned I02 source definitions and contexts:
donors: primary/secondary neutral amine; primary/secondary/tertiary carbon-bound
ammonium; NH2/NH1 amide; alcohol; phenol; pyrrolic NH.
acceptors: alcohol O; aldehyde/ketone/ester/amide carbonyl O;
primary/secondary/tertiary neutral amine; ether O; pyridine-like N.
New leg classes are these ten donor classes -> water O, water O -> these ten
acceptor classes, and water O -> water O (21 directed class families).
Existing I02 source manifests and explicit-H rule remain byte-identical.
This is not wholesale adoption of the broader ProLIF SMARTS role domains.

I12: a simple path between two distinct non-water endpoint atoms, through
exactly ONE identified water O, with both incident legs supported in the same
immutable chemical/coordinate/correspondence state. Endpoint atoms can each
be donor or acceptor under the specified roles; leg donor direction is retained
independently of the undirected connectivity of the bridge.
I13: the same proposition through 2..K distinct identified water O atoms,
where K is an explicit finite requested search bound (not an empirical cutoff).
Only water vertices are allowed internally. No non-water transit, repeated
water, cross-state edge, or shortcut through another endpoint is permitted.
The existing path engine supplies simple edge paths; retain distinct H tuples
and correspondence alternatives, not merely a binary residue-pair fingerprint.
Report water count and each leg. Any sum of leg distances is a path descriptor,
not endpoint separation or interaction strength.

Joint orientation means the very same supplied state and atom correspondence,
not separately optimized leg geometries. No water rotation is performed.
Retain bifurcated candidates if the same H satisfies both leg definitions:
the chosen source does not impose hydrogen capacity or one-contact exclusivity.
Do not interpret retained candidates as simultaneously occupied independent bonds.
Do not introduce lone-pair directions, tetrahedral windows or a new water model.

3. SUPPORTED DOMAIN
Explicit atom identities, supplied complete covalent topology/H/charge state,
unique resolved component-to-structure correspondence, qualified angstrom frame,
and exact non-water role definitions above. Multiple distinct waters and
disconnected supplied components remain distinct even with similar labels.
Prepared H coordinates are allowed ONLY as supplied, source-attributed model
coordinates. They must never be described as experimentally observed H positions.
Every result is relative to its explicitly selected finite state/event universe.

4. EXCLUSIONS / UNKNOWN CASES
No O-only water heuristic, H placement/rotation, protonation inference,
hydronium/hydroxide/peroxide/metal-bound oxygen substitution, or HOH-name typing.
No sulfur/carboxylate/pyridinium or other unqualified non-water role expansion.
Missing H/coordinates/charges/topology, degenerate angles, ambiguous mappings,
incomplete water inventory, unresolved alternate states, unqualified leg sources,
conflicting assertions or truncated enumeration cannot establish absence.
Exclude cross-component covalent legs and within-water self-legs. A broken source
binding/hash is an integrity failure, not an absent bridge.
Separate alternate water orientations must be evaluated as separate complete
states. Two individually possible legs in different orientations cannot compose.
