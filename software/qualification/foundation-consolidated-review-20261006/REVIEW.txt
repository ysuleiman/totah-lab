CONSOLIDATED FOUNDATION SCIENTIFIC GATES — 2026-10-06

This package consolidates the 40 remaining SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW_REQUIRED rows after
F07. It does not alter their status, invent receipts, or authorize a new framework.
The accepted capability ledger remains the master. Each family JSON preserves the
exact existing dossier definition, source-map pin, empirical requirements and
known limitations. Absence of a dossier is explicit, not filled with an invented
scientific citation. Existing R01/R02 and completed capabilities are not reopened.

qualified events, preserve alternatives, and carry an evaluated mask with every zero.

must be checked prospectively against existing transport, not assumed.
