LIMITATIONS
- Qualified source MolecularGraph correspondence and chemistry are required on both
  candidate sides; coordinate-only protein/SAM inputs do not automatically meet this.
- Domain excludes hetero/fused/other aromatic rings and unqualified ionic motifs.
- The existing OCL sanitizer can reject net-ion components. Its behavior is not changed
  or bypassed: failed component qualification is recorded; those atoms cannot support a
  scientific positive or a complete negative. A supported candidate in another valid
  component may still be assessed. General net-ion support is not claimed.
- Legacy protein charge templates are not sufficient by themselves. This version's
  supported native charged-group types are AMINE and CARBOXYLATE, corroborated by OCL
  patterns, exact mapping and formal charges. Other types, including unmatched protein
  template groups, remain outside the qualified domain.
- HBOND domain is deliberately alcohol/carbonyl C/H/O chemistry, not all N/S donors or
  acceptors. Source covalent D-H must agree with the existing enumerator; a missed or
  differently paired donor blocks a negative instead of silently losing it.
- Ring collector retains existing native geometry; newly requested measurements (e.g.
  both directional offsets) are not invented from historical payloads.
- Complete absence concerns the two explicit partner selections, never the full unqueried
  system. Unrecognized chemistry or insufficient historical fields are not evidence of absence.
