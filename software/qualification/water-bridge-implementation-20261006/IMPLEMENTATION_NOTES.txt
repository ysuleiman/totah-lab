I12 -> I13: implemented bounded proposition

Explicit-source-H water-mediated structural candidate. I12 has exactly one
internal neutral water; I13 has 2..K distinct internal neutral waters, with K
explicitly supplied. Every directional leg coexists in one immutable source
state. Donor direction need not coincide with path traversal direction.
Different source H tuples remain distinct. No energetic, occupancy, persistence,
physical solvent completeness, or universal water-bridge claim is made.

The versioned candidate universe is every source oxygen that is qualified water
or cannot be conclusively excluded using nonconflicting authoritative identity
facts. E1-E6 are defined and pinned in WATER_CANDIDATE_UNIVERSE_V1.txt. Fewer than
two visible H atoms exclude water only with a complete authoritative total-H
count; partial topology with missing H remains unknown. Inventory coverage proves
exhaustiveness only. It cannot establish chemistry, H orientation, role/leg
eligibility, or current scientific qualification.

WaterBridgeInputs validates explicit plan/artifact selection and uses existing
source-report replay and coverage checks. WaterIdentity applies the approved
predicate. WaterBridgeLegs reuses existing role occurrences, source-H attribution
and continuous D-A/H-A/DHA geometry. WaterBridgeRules passes qualified recomputed
same-state legs into unchanged EventPaths after excluding non-water transit.
No new generic graph, path, matcher, registry, routing, or evidence framework.
Existing public Java signatures are unchanged; only water-specific JSON schemas
and opt-in manifests were added. The HbondCandidateEnumeration hydrogen helper's
visibility changes from private to package scope without changing its body.

Neutral water has its own exact group definition. Ten previously reviewed
non-water donors and ten acceptors are reused, with water-water as the 21st
class family. No pyridinium, sulfur, carboxylate, or other role expansion.
The existing inclusive D-A <=3.5 angstrom / DHA >=130 degree candidate window is
used without an added epsilon, H-A cutoff or inter-leg angle. The simultaneous
boundary fixture uses representable coordinates; no production tolerance was
introduced to make a floating-point fixture pass.

All packaged manifests remain NOT_EVALUATED. Synthetic Research Gate fixtures
test valid and invalid governed execution without issuing any real production
review/receipt. Source envelope attribution is preserved, including a synthetic
mixed experimental-O/prepared-H provenance witness. Prepared H is not relabelled
as observed H. Raw measurement replay recomputes chemistry and geometry and
rejects altered measurements/definitions/state bindings.

The initial proposal's 50 named cases map to executable checks in
MATRIX_EXECUTION.json. The clarification supersedes W07's original blanket
negative wording: a nonconflicting E5/E6 exclusion is definite non-water and
therefore does not block inventory coverage; contradictory evidence remains
unresolved. C08 is exercised through conflicting source chemistry and rejected
measurement tampering because this contract recomputes legs rather than importing
external leg assertions. Generic contradictory-event behavior remains separately
covered by unchanged EventPathAcceptanceTest.

No external reference dataset, empirical distribution, unreviewed threshold,
implicit-H orientation, inferred water or PLIP heuristic/pruning policy was added.
The 12 external-data requirements remain separate. Source code remains subject
to the linked clean-source checkpoint; this note is not a scientific receipt.
