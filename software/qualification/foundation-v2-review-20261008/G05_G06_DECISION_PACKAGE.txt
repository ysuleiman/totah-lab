Recommendation: choose ChEBI:142094 as the FIRST exact domain. CCD net-zero SAM

Literature L05: Comparative Analysis of Sulfonium-pi, Ammonium-pi, and Sulfur-pi
Interactions and Relevance to SAM-dependent Methyltransferases. Existing pinned

https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules23112965, pinned L06.txt beside L05.txt.

  isotope identity; no isotope removal;

  side-chain C, ribose-linked C. The transferable methyl anchor is the unique

A. ATHENA.G06.SAM_CATECHOLATE_NAC_2018_SUMMARY/1 (candidate proposal)
Selected admitted SAM and an exact unsubstituted catechol monoanion:
[O-]c1ccccc1O. Ring has six aromatic carbons; adjacent O substituents, exactly one
O- and one neutral OH; no other substituents; closed shell. Only the O- is the
selected target. H counts/state are explicit; H coordinates do not enter geometry.
Predicate d(Cm,O) < 3.2 A AND angle(S,Cm,O) >= 165 degrees. Angle vertex is Cm,
using vectors Cm->S and Cm->O. No S...O or donor-distance substitute.
This preserves the 2018 inclusive summary as requested, with no original-boundary
parity claim. Original strict >165 may instead be selected as a distinct contract.

Remaining exact blocker before implementing A: source admission for a catecholate
in a coordination context. The COMT domain involves metal coordination. Ordinary
covalent identity cannot establish complete coordination coverage, and excluding
all metal-associated partners would sever the direct modeled COMT context. A
source-bound protocol must distinguish explicitly recorded O/Mg connections from
missing connectivity and must specify their admission without inferring valence,
coordination function or a complete first sphere. This package does NOT resolve
that question by extending S1 or inventing coordination. A's geometry definition
is explicit; its complete scientific admission contract is not yet supportable.
Neutral catechol, dinitrocatecholate, arbitrary phenolate, carbonyl O, N/S targets
and generic 'oxygen nucleophile' are not silently admitted.
