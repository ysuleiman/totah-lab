MAPPING FINDING
The first atom/bond-order permutation test failed when the adapter trusted OCL's valueAtomIndexes hint. OCL's fragment ordering need not follow the original bond endpoint order. The adapter now partitions the source at the toolkit-selected cut and uses OCL canonical identities of capped components to prove which side is constant/variable. It does not invent cuts or another identity algorithm. Toolkit subset copying marks a fragment as a query; the mapping verification explicitly restores the concrete capped-fragment flag to match MMP's concrete fragments. Membership that cannot be proved rejects the source.
Existing exhaustive core correspondence is then filtered to map the declared attachment anchors. Atom/bond mappings and symmetry alternatives are retained. This is structural correspondence, not a claim of unique physical atom lineage between historical molecules.

SCIENTIFIC EFFECT POLICY
Context equality requires exact target, endpoint, method, assay, conditions reference/hash, units, scale and qualification reference. Pairing also requires same study/group and source-bound observation references. IC50 and Ki, cellular and biochemical assays, different targets/conditions/units cannot silently pool. No automatic unit conversion, logarithm or endpoint harmonization is hidden in the analyzer.
Delta is right minus left on the declared scale. Increase/decrease is not benefit. The benchmark uses provided pChEMBL IC50 values (pIC50), not linear nM differences. Inputs preserve their original relation and bounds.
Supported relations: exact point, lower/upper censoring or interval. Invalid/inconsistent relations reject. Delta bounds are [right.lower-left.upper,right.upper-left.lower]. Open censoring bounds use conservative closed envelopes; boundary cases may abstain. Exact-delta arithmetic overflow rejects. Censored effects remain visible and are excluded from point means; no midpoint imputation or fabricated error bar.
Unresolved multiple measurements per molecule/context/study abstain rather than form Cartesian replicate pairs. Symmetry/site derivations do not multiply the same empirical comparison. Unconditional summaries also deduplicate observation pairs across alternative chemical interpretations while retaining the underlying effects.
Summary reports pair count, known study count, known exact-study count, study-balanced mean of exact deltas and sample SD of exact study means only when >=2 known studies and no unresolved-study group contributes. No confidence interval, significance claim or universal confidence score. Pairs sharing compounds are dependent. Unknown study provenance is marked UNRESOLVED:...; it never increments known independent-study count, and suppresses study SD. Such a mean is descriptive only.
Tolerance is explicit:0.3 pIC50 units in this diagnostic. WITHIN_TOLERANCE is a descriptive interval statement, not demonstrated biological equivalence or statistical non-significance.
