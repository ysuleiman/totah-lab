2026-10-06. Prepared after I02/F05/F14/F18 adoption; no runtime group was activated.

The existing RDKit FunctionalGroups source (pinned below) contains specific imine
subsets rather than one universal imine rule. Proposed first bounded domains are:

1. IMINE.N_METHYL.CARBON_BOUND: neutral, nonaromatic C=N-C(H3).
   Members: imine C and N. N-methyl carbon and other one-bond neighbors are context.
   N has exactly two heavy neighbors, H0; methyl context carbon has one heavy neighbor,
   authoritative H3. Imine C has 0/1/2 carbon single-bond substituents and respectively
   authoritative H2/H1/H0. Heteroatom substitutions and aromatic/endocyclic imine N
   are outside this first definition. N-methyl identity must reuse the approved methyl
   predicate/correspondence, not infer H3 from an OCL match alone.

2. IMINE.METHYLENE.CARBON_BOUND: neutral, nonaromatic R(C)-N=CH2.
   Members: imine C and N. Carbon R and one-bond neighbors are context.
   C has only N as heavy neighbor and authoritative H2; N has exactly two heavy
   neighbors and H0. The source C-N attachment is SINGLE. Aromatic/endocyclic N and
   heteroatom-attached/formaldehyde-imine variants are outside this first definition.
   The two imine subset identities may overlap; do not destructively choose a label.

3. OXIME.HYDROXY.CARBON_BOUND: neutral nonaromatic C=N-O-H.
   Members: C/N/O; carbon substituents and explicit H remain attributed context.
   N has two heavy neighbors/H0; O has one heavy neighbor/H1. C has 0/1/2 carbon
   single-bond substituents and H2/H1/H0. O-alkyl oxime ethers are not this exact
   hydroxy identity. No tautomerization, E/Z assignment or oxime stereochemistry
   inference. Supplied E/Z state is preserved without claiming chemical equivalence.

4. NITROSO.CARBON_BOUND: neutral nonaromatic R(C)-N=O.
   Members: N/O; attachment carbon and one-bond neighbors remain context.
   N has two heavy neighbors/H0; O one heavy neighbor/H0. Carbon-N is SINGLE.
   Nitrite R-O-N=O and nitro charge-separated N/O are distinct representations;
   do not normalize them into this identity.

Each proposed identity requires authoritative complete graph/mapping/charge/H state
and exact bond orders. Charged/resonance alternatives outside the reviewed state
remain unsupported/inconclusive, not normalized. No lone-pair, donor/acceptor,
reactivity, pKa, covalent inhibition, toxicity, affinity or biological claim.
Symmetry-equivalent mappings are alternatives of a member-set occurrence; genuinely
different occurrences survive. Overlaps with existing identities remain independent.

REVIEW DECISION REQUIRED

Approval is needed before production qualification. No public API/schema is proposed:
