E/Z: ONE EXCLUSION CLASS, 18 COMPOUNDS

parity 2 (Zor2), CIP parity 2 (ZorM, here Z), smallRing=false. Each also has
one endocyclic small-ring C=N with relative parity 1 and CIP parity 0. The
latter is topology-related and is not the exclusion trigger. Full individual
IDs, exact slash/backslash SMILES and bond tables are in diagnostics.
OCL parses and distinguishes explicit imine E/Z; the control tests prove it.
MolecularGraph stores an unstructured bond stereo string but has no accepted
E/Z reference-substituent/direction semantics. fromOcl emits UNSPECIFIED for
bonds; toOcl rejects descriptors other than NONE/UNSPECIFIED. The source
round-trip guard correctly rejects non-small-ring bond parity before this
loss can enter production. JSON preserves the graph string but cannot restore
source information lost upstream. Identity/correspondence depend on the
mapper. Sanitization also needs review: stripping stereo when all atom stereo
is unspecified is not safe for future bond-only stereo support.
Primary limitation: graph semantic contract + mapper, NOT OCL parsing or JSON.
No stereo support extension implemented. A reviewed public stereo contract,
mapper, correspondence and sanitizer characterization are prerequisites.
