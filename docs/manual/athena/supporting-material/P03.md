# P03: bounded donor/acceptor role composition

## Scientific definition and reasoning

The authoritative definitions are the 17 `perception-foundation-v1` manifests rendered
in [rule records](../RULES.md). Each role requires both a B00 graph occurrence and
explicit source-state coverage. Neutral amine and ammonium states are distinct;
amide N-H donor and carbonyl O acceptor identities may coexist on separate atoms.
Pyrrole, pyridine and pyridinium are supplied chemical states, not tautomers selected
by the analyzer. Explicit H supplies atom correspondence; implicit H does not invent
coordinates. Absence means absence of one reviewed class, never all donor/acceptor chemistry.

## Decisions, evidence strength and limitations

ADOPT graph/state attribution and immutable role evidence. MODIFY coarse external
features into bounded state-specific classes. REJECT role-name-to-interaction inference
and global absence from an empty set of positives. UNSUPPORTED sulfur/water, organic-F,
acid-OH acceptor and unreviewed chemical domains remain excluded. Unknown H remains
inconclusive. No new empirical dataset, fitted distribution, threshold or calibration
is needed for this composition check; synthetic fixtures exercise already defined
constitutional/state predicates and report coexistence, not biological validity.

The dossiers are research drafts. They explicitly identify unavailable full-text support
for class-specific NH directional windows. Those windows are outside this checkpoint.
No research eligibility, reviewer validity period or current-policy certificate is
invented. Thus P03's bounded composition implementation is closed, while universal
perception and current-policy scientific qualification remain unclaimed. Exact source
and implementation hashes are in the composition checkpoint's SOURCE_SHA256SUMS.
