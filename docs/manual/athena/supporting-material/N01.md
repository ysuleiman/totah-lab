# N01: role-to-geometry attribution

This is bounded engineering qualification of existing evidence interfaces, not a new
scientific interaction rule. [P03](P03.md) supplies the role definitions and caveats.

The accepted [inventory](../../../../software/qualification/rule-qualification-blueprint-20261005/REFERENCE_CAPABILITY_INVENTORY.json), entry N01, identifies ProLIF role inversion/metadata and PLIP atom mapping as precedents and pins their source versions. Athena adopts exact partner attribution; inverse role labels do not create an independent mechanism. RING executable-source absence remains recorded in that inventory. No external implementation equivalence is claimed.

The fixture resolves acetamide donor N and acceptor O through the existing component
correspondence, explicitly asserts that the fixture has one mapping, and passes those
AtomReferences to `athena.geometry/1`. Group source references link the unchanged role
envelopes. Reversing the ordered vector reverses its direction while preserving length
and the pair distance. EvidenceExchange retains role and geometry payloads byte-for-byte.

No automatic role-to-plan adapter, ambiguous-mapping selection, D-H placement, H-bond
classification, network aggregation or new schema is introduced. The synthetic
intramolecular coordinates are arithmetic witnesses, not a physical conformation.
Geometry validates its explicit plan/state; it does not independently authenticate the
scientific truth of a referenced role. Unknown role domains remain unknown. No global
absence or current-policy eligibility follows from this test.

Implementation owners: SystemStateView.Component correspondence; FunctionalGroupRules;
ContinuousGeometryRules; EvidenceExchange. Exact definitions remain in their manifests.
[Checkpoint](../../../../software/qualification/n01-role-geometry-attribution-20261005/CHECKPOINT.txt)
contains hashes and reproducible test/replay commands. This bounded path needs no fitted
threshold, literature-derived distribution or new dataset. Broader family attribution
remains pending; this is not closure of all N01 domains.
